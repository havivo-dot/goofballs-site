Goofballs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Goofballs is a casual game made for everyone, including kids. We designed it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing personal. Goofballs has no accounts, no chat, no analytics SDK, and does not track you across apps or websites. The name you type at the start is used only on your device to generate a creature; it never leaves your phone.

Game progress

Your coins, Goofballs and settings are stored on your device. If you use Game Center leaderboards or achievements, Apple processes that data under Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising

Goofballs shows ads served by Google AdMob. Because the game is made for all ages, we ask Google to treat the app as child‑directed: ads are non‑personalized and the advertising identifier is not used. The ad SDK may process technical data (such as IP address, device type and ad interactions) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from AdMob apps. A one‑time purchase removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded videos remain available.

Purchases

In‑app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Optional local reminders (for example, "your free egg is ready") are scheduled on your device only. Turn them off any time in iOS Settings.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no social features, no user‑generated content that leaves the device, and no links to outside websites other than this policy and the ad provider's disclosures.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on our GitHub page. A support email will be added at launch.
